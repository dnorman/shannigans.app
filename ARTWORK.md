## Original prompt

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: full-bleed website hero background for Claude Shannigans, a macOS Claude Desktop account switcher.
Input image 1 is a reference for Claude Shannon's likeness, mature proportions, semi-realistic cartoon treatment, and the two account tiles. It is not a layout to copy.
Create a new wide landscape illustration, approximately 2:1, with a different relaxed pose: Shannon seated at a simple charcoal-edged desk, one elbow casually resting, smoothly tossing two small square identity tiles, utterly deadpan expression and one slightly raised eyebrow. Recognizably the same adult man, silver-streaked dark hair, charcoal tweed jacket, white shirt, narrow dark tie. Realistic editorial caricature, fine ink contours and subtle painted detail, not chibi, not a photo.
One pale tile clearly reads exactly "bob-0" in charcoal. One raspberry-red tile clearly reads exactly "bob-1" in white. Both tiles must be large enough to read, with a restrained sense of motion. No other words, logos, letters, captions, speech bubbles, equations, UI or watermarks.
Composition: character, face, hands and both tiles all fit in the RIGHT HALF of the scene, with generous headroom and side margins so neither is clipped in responsive crops. The LEFT 45 percent is quiet uninterrupted negative space for live website typography. A continuous light cool mint background fills the whole image edge to edge, consistent soft color, not beige or cream, no gradient, no vignette, no frame. Desk runs subtly across the very bottom. The composition is one unified scene, not a split panel or card. Bright enough for inspection; do not darken the portrait. Keep adult anatomy and expressive hands convincing.
Mood: dry, poised, understatedly absurd. No broad grin, no mascot cheerfulness, no visual explanation of the joke.
